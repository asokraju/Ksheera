# Rudhra's Word Workshop — 3-letter words (Kindergarten)

`Rudhras_Word_Workshop_3_Letter_Words.pdf` is a printable, 45-page book for reading 3-letter (CVC)
words, themed around what Rudhra loves: circuits and wires, solar panels, trains, cars and the school bus.

| Pages | What |
|---|---|
| 1–7 | Cover, how to teach (Sound it → Slide it → Say it), sound check, 4-week plan, daily notes, games |
| 8–9 | Vowel sounds poster, Solar Power progress chart |
| 10–34 | 20 days of activities: Robot Ears, Power the Bulb, Word Train, Park the Cars, Wire It Up, Fix-It Shop, Bus Stop, Build the Word, Read & Circle, Read & Draw, Yes or No, Reading Check |
| 35–43 | Cut-outs: 44 picture word cards, letter tiles, Robot Bingo (4 boards), certificate |
| 44–45 | Answer key |

Rebuild: `python3 build.py`. Pictures come from `common/icons.py`; change `NAME` at the top of `build.py`
to make a copy for another child.
